If you take a Recruited address

You can send from an address we issue instead of your own. If you do, there is no password for us to hold, because there is nothing of yours to authenticate as.

What we do hold is the address itself and the real address your replies go to. Your letters carry that on Reply-To, so an employer answering one writes straight to your own inbox — the reply does not come to us and we do not keep a copy.

The trade is that the letter leaves through our mail provider rather than your mailbox, so that provider processes it — the same as your own provider would.

The mail password, specifically

This section applies only if you connect your own mailbox. Doing that means giving this site something that can send mail as you, because there is no way to send as somebody without it. So, plainly:

Use an app password, not your account password. It can be revoked on its own, without changing your password or touching another device.

It is stored encrypted, with the key held in the server's environment and never in the database — so the most likely breach by a distance, a leaked database dump, yields ciphertext.

It is still access to your mailbox. An app password can generally read that mailbox as well as send from it. That is the honest summary, and it is why the by-hand route exists and always will: the app writes the letter, you press send from your own mail client, and nothing of yours is stored.

Disconnecting the mailbox deletes the stored credential.

Cookies

One, and it is the sign-in session. There is no analytics, no tracking pixel and no third-party script on any page of this site.

How long it is kept

While you have an account. Delete your account and it goes — the user row, the profile, the CV, the drafts, the contacted list and the stored mail credential, in one operation, from your account page. It is not a request queued for somebody to action; the rows are removed.

What cannot be pulled back: letters already sent are in the recipient's inbox, and Stripe keeps its own payment records for as long as its own obligations require.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask for a copy of what is held, ask for it to be corrected, ask for it to be deleted, ask for it in a portable form, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent for automatic sending at any time.

4 Sending rules — optional

These are already set sensibly. Change them only if you want to.

{{ config.MAX_DAILY_CAP }} a day is as high as we would go. There are rarely more fresh jobs than that worth writing to anyway.

A wider window fills a quiet queue, but a job posted three weeks ago has already had three hundred applicants.

{# Ticked state is derived from the number itself rather than stored, so somebody who set 60 comes back to a form that still says 60 instead of one that quietly resets them. #} {% set over = settings.daily_cap > config.MAX_DAILY_CAP %}

Read this first. Sending more than {{ config.MAX_DAILY_CAP }} cold emails a day from a personal address is how that address gets scored as a spammer. It is not only these letters that suffer: once your domain is marked, everything you send starts landing in spam, including your reply to the employer who did want to talk to you.

Gmail also cuts a free account off at 500 a day and locks it for 24 hours; Outlook at 300. And the extra letters go to generic inboxes, which reply at about 10% against 38% for a named person — so past this point you are trading your reputation for a worse reply rate.

Your mailbox, your call. Untick this and anything above {{ config.MAX_DAILY_CAP }} drops back to {{ config.MAX_DAILY_CAP }}.

The wait is your undo: a letter can be stopped from the drafts screen until it goes.

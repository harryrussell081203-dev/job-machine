What does matter

  • How old the advert is. Anything over a week has a shortlist already. Under 48 hours is the window worth chasing.
  • Who receives it. On the same set of emails, moving from a generic inbox to a named person was worth about four times the reply rate. That is the lever. The clock is not.

If you want a rule anyway

Tuesday to Thursday morning, because Monday is triage and Friday is gone. Treat it as a tie-breaker for when you were going to send anyway, not as a reason to sit on a finished letter for two days. A letter that waits until Tuesday is competing against a shortlist that formed on Monday.

From those 87 replies came 12 interviews, rounds and phone invitations included, and 3 job offers, all in writing. "Replied" counts polite noes too, because a no from a person still means the email reached one. Automatic replies and bounces are not counted.

What it means for your own search

  • Most emails get no reply, and that is normal. Even the good months were about one in four.
  • The address mattered more than anything else. Guessing cost 132 bounces and a damaged inbox. Using only addresses employers print took replies from 9% to 23%.
  • It takes weeks, not days. One offer came 17 days after a single short email.
  • Volume of good applications wins. 12 interviews needed hundreds of real, specific emails, not a handful of perfect ones.

These are one person's figures, so treat them as a guide, not a promise. The live figures are published, including the bad months.

The honest version

Plenty of employers do screen applications automatically, and plenty of advice exists about beating it — keyword stuffing, plain formatting, matching the job description word for word. Some of that helps a little.

But it aims at the wrong problem. Whether a machine read your application badly or no human read it at all, the outcome is identical and the fix is the same.

A portal application is filed, not delivered

Nobody at the company is notified that it arrived. There is no thread, no inbox, no obligation and nothing for anyone to reply to. It sits in a system until somebody chooses to go and look, which may be never.

An email to a person behaves completely differently. It lands in an inbox belonging to somebody whose job is to deal with what is in it, and not answering is a small active decision rather than the default.

Do both

Apply through the portal as well. It costs five minutes, it is where the formal process lives, and the email is what makes anyone go and look at it.

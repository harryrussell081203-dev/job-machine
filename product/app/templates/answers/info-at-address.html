The hiring-inbox row is only ten emails, so read 50% as "about as good as a named person", not as better. The row that is solid is the bottom one: 42 emails is a real sample and 10% is a real result.

Why it is so much worse

Nobody in particular is responsible for reading info@. It collects supplier enquiries, complaints and spam, and the person who empties it has no authority to do anything about a job. Your letter is not competing with other applicants there — it is competing with a printer cartridge invoice.

What to do instead, in ten minutes

  1. Read the advert to the very end. Addresses printed there replied 6 times out of 9.
  2. The company's own /contact, /careers, /about and /team pages. 77 addresses came from there, replying at 21%.
  3. A hiring inbox if no name is going.

When info@ is all there is

Send it. 10% is worse than 38% and still better than a portal, which has nobody behind it at all. Just do not spend a tailored hour on it, and do not follow it up twice.

Where to find someone to write to

The company's own website: /contact, /careers, /about, /team. Smaller firms often list the whole team with addresses. A careers@ or hr@ inbox is fine too; an info@ inbox is read by nobody in particular and replies far less.

Type the company's name here and it checks those pages for you and lists what the company publishes. It never guesses.

What to write

  • The work you do and the one kind of role you want. Not "any role".
  • Two things you have done, with figures.
  • One question: "Do you take on people for this kind of work?"
  • Your name, phone number, and your CV attached.

60 to 90 words. Nobody reads a long letter from a stranger about a job that does not exist yet.

If nothing comes back

One short follow-up after 5 working days, on the same thread. Then move on. The follow-up calculator works out the dates.

The rules, and why

  • Eight words or fewer. A phone shows about that many in the inbox. Anything longer is cut off before it says what it is about.
  • The role, exactly as the advert names it. A manager filling three vacancies sorts by title. "Maintenance technician" matches; "engineering opportunity" makes them open it to find out.
  • One detail that proves you read the advert. The site, the team or the kit: "Aberdeen harbour", "night shift, packaging lines".
  • Never "Application for". It reads as a form letter from somebody applying everywhere.
  • No capitals, no exclamation marks, no "URGENT". Those are what spam filters and tired readers both look for.

On a follow-up

Reply on the same thread so the subject becomes "Re:" plus the original. A new subject on a follow-up reads as a new stranger, and they have to go and find what you sent before.

Why guessing fails

Most advice on this search tells you to guess a pattern such as firstname.lastname@company.co.uk and run it through a checker. Never guess. Plenty of companies use another pattern, the person has left, or the checker says "maybe". Each wrong guess bounces, and mail providers read a run of bounces as a spammer. That spam score sits on your own inbox, so the next real application, to an address that does exist, is more likely to land in spam too.

Where real addresses are

  • The advert itself. Often at the bottom, in the small print.
  • The company's own website: /contact, /careers, /about, /team. Small firms often list the whole team.
  • A careers@ or hr@ inbox is fine. An info@ inbox works, but is read by nobody in particular and replies far less.

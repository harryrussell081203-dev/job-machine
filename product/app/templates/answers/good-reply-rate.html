The benchmark, and what happened

Cold outreach is normally quoted at 1% to 5%. Across 86 cold emails to real UK employers over four weeks, 22 came back. That is 26%.

The letters were near identical to each other. The variable that moved was who opened them.

Three things before you compare your own

  • A reply is not an interview. Plenty of those 22 were polite noes. Counting a rejection as a win is how a number improves while things get worse.
  • A rate without its count is not worth reading. 50% here is five replies out of ten. It is in the table because leaving it out would be dishonest, not because it is a result.
  • This is one person's log in engineering and technician roles in Scotland. A different trade may behave differently.

What a bad rate usually means

Not the writing. On this sample the gap between the best recipient and the worst was about four times, which no amount of rewriting closes. If replies are not coming, look first at who the letters went to, then at whether the adverts were fresh, and only then at the letter itself.

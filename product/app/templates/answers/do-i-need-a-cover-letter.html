The short version works better

A hiring manager with a pile of applications gives each one a few seconds. A page-long letter gets skimmed; a short email gets read to the end. What it has to do is prove you read the advert and give a reason to open the CV.

What goes in it

  1. The exact role and one detail from the advert: the site, the team, the kit. "Maintenance technician on your Dyce packaging lines."
  2. Two things you have done that matter for this job, with a figure if you have one.
  3. One question. One, so answering is easy.
  4. Name, phone number, CV attached. The same sign-off every time.

Where to send it

To a person, not only the Apply button. An address printed in the advert or on the company's own website is where replies come from. Type the company's name here to find one.

Before you send it, check it does not read like AI wrote it.

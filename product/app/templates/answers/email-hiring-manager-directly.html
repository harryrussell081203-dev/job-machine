What makes it welcome

  • An address they published. In the advert, on their website's contact or team page, or a hiring inbox like careers@. Printing an address is an invitation to use it.
  • Short. 60 to 90 words: the role, one detail from the advert, two things you have done that matter to it, one question.
  • Once. One email to one person at the employer. At most one short follow-up on the same thread if nothing comes back.

What makes it unwelcome

  • A guessed address. If it bounces, it hurts your email's reputation; if it lands on the wrong person, it wastes their time.
  • Writing to three people at the same company about one job.
  • A long cover letter pasted into the email body.
  • Chasing again and again.

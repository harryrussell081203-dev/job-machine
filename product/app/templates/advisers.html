Something free to hand the people you work with

For careers services, job clubs and employment teams. Free for them and for you: no account, nothing to install, nothing to sign.

What it does

Most applications go through a job portal and are sorted by software. An email to a real person at the company is read by a person. The hard part is finding the address.

Type a company's name or paste a job advert at recruited.org.uk/find. It reads the advert and the company's own website and shows the real address the company publishes, best first, and can write a short letter to it. It never guesses an address, and says plainly when there isn't one.

What it will not do

  • Guess an email address, or write to anybody on its own.
  • Ask for an account, a CV or contact details to use the finder.
  • Sell or pass on anything typed into it. See privacy.

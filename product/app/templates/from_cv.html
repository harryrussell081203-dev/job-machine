Reading a CV into the profile has worked for weeks. It was wired to the wrong screen: it prefilled /profile, which is seven panels and about twenty boxes, and nothing is saved until the person reaches the bottom and presses save. One user has ever got this far. They uploaded a CV, landed on the prefilled form, and left. No profile was saved, so nothing ever ran for them. The reading was correct and the machine still did nothing, because filling a form in for somebody does not make it a short form. So this screen saves. It shows what was read as plain text to be checked - the qualifications especially, because a model reading a CV is the one thing in this product that can invent a credential - and asks only for what a CV cannot say: the least they will work for, whether they are in a job now, and how far they will go. Everything else rides along hidden and is parsed by the same _profile_from_form the full form uses. "Change something" posts the whole reading into the full form, so nobody pays for a second read of the CV to fix one word. #}

What to write

Short gets read. 60 to 90 words:

  1. The job you're after, and one thing about {{ page.company }} that shows you looked: a site, a product, something from their advert.
  2. Two things you've actually done that matter for this job, with a number if you have one.
  3. One question, so replying is easy.

Then your name, your phone number and "CV attached". Subject line: the job title, nothing else. Why.

Use an app password, not your normal one. Your real password will be refused here even when it is correct, because Google and Microsoft both block ordinary sign-ins from apps.

Gmail: Google Account → Security → 2-Step Verification → App passwords. Outlook: Security → Advanced security options → App passwords.

Be clear on what this gives us. An app password lets this app send mail as you, and it would also let anyone holding it read that mailbox. It is stored encrypted, and the key is not in the database — but the honest summary is that you are trusting this app with your mailbox.

You can revoke it at any moment from your email provider, without changing your own password or touching any other device. If you would rather not, skip this: the app still writes every letter and you send them in one click each.

Two ways to do this

Your own address gets more replies. A letter from {{ profile.get('email') or user['email'] }} reads as a person applying for a job, because it is one. That is the whole reason these get answered, and it is why it is the first option here.

Use your own mailbox

{% endif %}

Use an app password, not your normal one. Your real password is refused even when it is correct.

Gmail: Google Account → Security → 2-Step Verification → App passwords. Outlook: Security → Advanced security options → App passwords.

What this gives us. An app password lets us send mail as you, and would let anyone holding it read that mailbox. It is stored encrypted, but the honest summary is that you are trusting us with your mailbox.

Revoke it any time from your email provider, without changing your own password. Or skip this — we still write every letter and you press send.

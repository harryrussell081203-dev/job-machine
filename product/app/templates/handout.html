Who do you actually email about this job?

Clicking Apply puts your CV in a pile that software sorts. An email to a real person at the company gets read by a person. This finds you the address.

  1. Go to recruited.org.uk/find on your phone or a computer.
  2. Type the company's name, or paste the job advert.
  3. You get the real email address the company publishes, best first, and a short letter you can send from your own email.

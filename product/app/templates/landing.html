Almost nobody writes to the address printed at the bottom of the advert, because everybody clicks Apply. Finding that person is the part this does for you.

What it does

Finds fresh jobs. Under 48 hours old, matched to the work you want and how far you will travel.

Digs out a real address. From the advert, then the company's own site. Never guessed. No real address, no email.

Writes the letter. 60–90 words, one detail from that advert, one question. Your CV attached.

You press send. One click each, from your own address.

What it will never do

Guess an email address. Claim a qualification you do not hold. Write to the same employer twice. Name your current employer to anybody.

Questions

Is this spam?

One short letter to one person, about a job they advertised. Never the same employer twice, and a daily limit you set.

Does it send from my email?

Yes, once you connect it, so replies come straight to you. Or leave it unconnected: it writes the letters and you press send.

I am in work. Will my employer find out?

Add them to "Never write to" and nothing ever reaches anyone there, whatever name an advert uses. Letters never name your current employer.

Do you guess email addresses?

Never. Only an address printed in the advert or on the company's own site. No real address, no letter.

Can I cancel?

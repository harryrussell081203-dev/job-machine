Questions

What do I need?

Four answers, your name, phone and email. That’s it to start. A CV is optional; add one any time and it goes with every email after that. To send from your own email you connect it later, in a minute.

What jobs does it work for?

Any job in the UK that gets advertised: warehouse, care, driving, trades, kitchens, offices. Nothing in it is built around one trade.

Why email a person instead of clicking Apply?

When you click Apply, your CV joins a pile and a computer sorts it. When you email the person hiring, a human reads it. Almost nobody does that, so you stand out.

The hard part is the address. When Harry guessed addresses, {{ proof.guessed.bounce_pct }}% bounced ({{ proof.guessed.bounced }} of {{ proof.guessed.sent }}). Using only real ones, {{ proof.published.bounce_pct }}% did ({{ proof.published.bounced }} of {{ proof.published.sent }}). So we only use an address the company has put online itself.

Is this spam?

No. It is one short email to one person, about a job they advertised. Never the same company twice, and you choose how many a day.

Does it send from my email?

Yes, if you connect it, so replies come straight to you. Or don't connect it: we write the emails and you press send.

What is autopilot?

Once your email is connected, it sends each email by itself. By default you get an hour to stop any of them first (you can change that), and you set a daily limit.

I have a job now. Will my boss find out?

Add your employer to “never write to” and nothing ever reaches anyone there. Emails never name your current job's company.

Do you guess email addresses?

Never. Only an address printed in the advert or on the company's own website. No real address, no email.

How these are counted

Every application email in Harry's sent mail since 1 May, counted on {{ proof.counted_on }}: every letter, not a good fortnight chosen out of it. "People wrote back" includes polite noes, because a no from a person still means the email reached one; automatic replies and bounces are not counted.

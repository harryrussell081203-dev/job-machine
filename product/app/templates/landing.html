Why email a person?

When you click Apply, your CV joins a pile of hundreds and a computer sorts it. When you email the person hiring, a human reads it. Almost nobody does that, so you stand out.

The hard part is finding the right email address. Guessing does not work. When Harry guessed addresses, almost half of his emails bounced back. Using only real ones, almost none did, and far more people replied:

Questions

Is this spam?

No. It is one short email to one person, about a job they advertised. Never the same company twice, and you choose how many a day.

Does it send from my email?

Yes, if you connect it, so replies come straight to you. Or don't connect it: we write the emails and you press send.

What is autopilot?

Once your email is connected, it sends each email by itself. By default you get an hour to stop any of them first (you can change that), and you set a daily limit.

Do I need a CV?

No. It works without one. Add a CV whenever you like and it goes with every email after that.

I have a job now. Will my boss find out?

Add your employer to “never write to” and nothing ever reaches anyone there. Emails never name your current job's company.

Do you guess email addresses?

Never. Only an address printed in the advert or on the company's own website. No real address, no email.

How these are counted

Every application email in Harry's sent mail since 1 May, counted on {{ proof.counted_on }}: every letter, not a good fortnight chosen out of it. "People wrote back" includes polite noes, because a no from a person still means the email reached one; automatic replies and bounces are not counted. Replies that arrived as a new email are missed, so it is a floor. And a reply is not the same thing as an interview. The {{ proof.interviews }} interviews are his own count, rounds and phone invitations included; the written invitations in his inbox alone come from {{ proof.interview_employers }} employers. The offers are all in writing. In May addresses were guessed from a pattern; from August only addresses employers publish were used.

Sending more makes it worse

Every other tool in this market sells volume. The 2026 numbers say volume is the problem, not the solution:

  • The average application reaches interview about 2–3% of the time.
  • People who sent 11–20 applications in a quarter interviewed at 9.25%. People who sent 100 or more managed 2.58% — less than a third. (Huntr, Q1 2026)
  • A bulk auto-apply tool, tested independently: 200 applications, one callback. 0.5%.

Applying to more jobs did not just fail to help. It actively hurt, because a hundred applications can only be a hundred untailored ones, and a recruiter spots that in under twenty seconds.

Not the same measurement as ours, deliberately said out loud.

## The app

- **Link:** https://recruited.org.uk. Add a tracking tag for the platform so I can see what works: `?c=reddit`, `?c=facebook`, `?c=instagram`, `?c=tiktok`, `?c=youtube` or `?c=x`. Example: https://recruited.org.uk/?c=reddit
- **What it does, in plain words:** you tell it the job you want, your town, your last job and the least you'd take. Every weekday it finds matching UK jobs. For each one it finds the real email address the employer publishes (in the advert or on their own website, never a guessed one). It writes a short email from you and sends it from your own email, with your CV. Or you check each one and press send. Replies come to your own inbox. Sign-up takes about a minute.
- **Why it matters:** clicking Apply puts your CV in a pile that a computer sorts. Emailing the person hiring gets read by a human. The hard part is finding a real address, and that's what it does.
- **Free things, no account needed:**
  - https://recruited.org.uk/find: type any company and see the real email addresses it publishes, or a straight "there are none".
  - https://recruited.org.uk/employers: how to email UK employers about a job.
  - https://recruited.org.uk/playbook: a free guide to emailing employers directly.
- **Price:** the first 100 people get it free for life. The front page shows how many free places are left; check it on the day, never guess. After that it's £9 a month, cancel any time, no charge per application.
- **Who it's for:** anyone job hunting in the UK. Warehouse, care, driving, trades, kitchens, retail, office, graduates. Nothing in it is built around one trade.

## Hard rules (never break these)

1. **My real accounts only.** Never create accounts and never use fake or second accounts. No posing as a "happy user". Every post is from me and says I built it, e.g. "I built this for my own job hunt".
2. **Never mention my age or my current employer.** Never name the employers that interviewed me or made the offers. Never say I "accepted" an offer, and don't date the offers.
3. **Only the numbers above.** No invented testimonials, user counts, success rates or guarantees.
4. **Never post anyone's personal email or name.** No hiring managers, no recruiters, nobody. Shared company inboxes like careers@ on the employers page are fine.
5. **No unsolicited messages to individuals.** No cold DMs and no emailing people. Only reply to someone who asked a question or commented on my post.
6. **Read each community's rules first.** If it bans self-promotion or links, either skip it or post only a genuinely helpful answer with no link, and tell me which you did. Never post the same text twice.
7. **Human pace.** At most 3 posts or link-replies a day across all platforms, spread out, and never more than one in the same community in a week. Being useful beats being frequent.
8. **Never run any of this from a Hydro Group device or network.** If you can tell that's where we are, stop and tell me.
